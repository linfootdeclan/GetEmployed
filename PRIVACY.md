# Privacy

This plugin is a set of written instructions (skills) for Claude. It contains no code that runs on its own, no hooks, no servers, and no analytics. The author does not receive, collect, or store any of your data.

## What the plugin handles

When you use it, Claude works with the information you give it and the accounts you have connected:

- **Your résumé and job preferences** (your Job Description profile). Used to search, score leads, and write tailored résumés and cover letters.
- **Your Gmail and Google Calendar**, if you connect them. Read-only for status checks. The plugin creates Gmail drafts and calendar events only when you ask, and never sends email.
- **Job sites and the web.** Search terms such as a job title, a place, and a company name go to the job-search connectors you have enabled (Indeed, JobsPipe) and to web search and page fetches. Your résumé, email, phone number, and address are not sent to these services as search terms.
- **Employer application pages.** Claude fills in an application only when you ask, using your résumé, and stops before the final step. You submit each application yourself. What you submit goes to that employer, under that employer's privacy policy.
- **Your files.** Résumés, cover letters, the tracker, and notes are saved in your Claude project and in a `Job Searching with Claude` folder in your Downloads folder.

## What it does not do

- It does not send data to the author or to any service the plugin itself operates.
- It does not send email, messages, or connection requests for you.
- It does not create accounts, enter passwords, or handle payment or ID numbers.
- It does not contact an employer or hiring contact without your approval.

## Where your data is stored

In your own Claude project and on your own computer. Claude itself is governed by Anthropic's privacy policy: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy. Each connector (Indeed, JobsPipe, Gmail, Google Calendar) is governed by its provider's policy.

## Deleting your data

Delete the project files and the `Job Searching with Claude` folder, and disconnect any connector in your Claude settings.
